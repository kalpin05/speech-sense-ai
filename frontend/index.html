About the Platform

Intelligent therapy,
powered by AI

Our platform uses advanced AI and machine learning to deliver personalized speech therapy at scale. Whether you're overcoming a stutter, refining accent, or recovering from an injury — we adapt to you.

Backed by certified speech-language pathologists and built with the latest research, SpeechAI combines clinical best practices with consumer-grade UX.
